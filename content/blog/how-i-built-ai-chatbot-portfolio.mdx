## Why I wanted an AI chatbot in my portfolio

When I started working on my portfolio, I didn't want it to be just another website with my name, skills, projects, and contact information.

A portfolio should tell people what I can build.

But I also wanted it to demonstrate something more practical — how I can work with modern AI technologies and integrate them into a real application.

That's why I decided to add an AI chatbot to my portfolio.

The idea was simple:

Instead of making visitors search through different sections of my portfolio, I wanted to give them another way to interact with it.

They could ask questions about my projects, skills, experience, or the technologies I work with.

That small idea became one of the more interesting parts of my portfolio.

---

## What I wanted the chatbot to do

I didn't want to build a chatbot just for the sake of having a chatbot.

The chatbot needed to have a purpose.

My basic goals were:

- Keep the interaction simple
- Make the chatbot feel like a natural part of the portfolio
- Connect it with a generative AI model
- Stream responses instead of making users wait for the complete response
- Keep the UI clean and responsive
- Make it useful for understanding my work

The important part for me was the integration.

I wanted to understand what actually happens between a user's message, the application, the AI model, and the response shown on the screen.

---

## The technology I used

For the application, I worked with:

- Next.js
- React
- TypeScript
- Vercel AI SDK
- Google Gemini
- Tailwind CSS

Next.js gave me the application structure and API capabilities, while the AI SDK helped simplify the communication between the application and the language model.

Gemini handled the actual generative AI part.
